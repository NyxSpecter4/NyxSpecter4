Privacy Policy

App: The Princetown Packet — Verifier's Run
Developer: Kiran Wolfe (Kinetigor)
Effective: November 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To save your progress, the app stores your chosen player name, your game progress, and your run receipts in your device's local browser storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share a run yourself.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage (or uninstall the app) to delete everything it saved.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
